# HSK3 Vocabulary — Words, Mnemonics & Example Sentences

Source: every word's pinyin, part of speech, and English meaning is taken
directly from the textbook's "生词/New Words" tables per lesson. Every example
sentence is drawn from the actual dialogue lines (using the textbook's own
"拼音课文/Texts in Pinyin" section for pinyin, which HSK3 uniquely provides).

**Important:** unlike HSK1/2, HSK3 does not provide official English
translations of dialogue lines — only word-level glosses. The English
translation of each example sentence below is mine, done carefully to match
the Chinese precisely. Mnemonics are memory aids, not textbook content.

## Lesson 1 — 周末你有什么打算 (What's your plan for the weekend)

- **周末** (zhōumò) — n. — weekend — *Sounds like "joe-mo" — imagine "Joe" taking it easy every weekend.*
  → 周末你有什么打算？— Zhōumò nǐ yǒu shénme dǎsuàn? — What are your plans for the weekend?

- **打算** (dǎsuàn) — n./v. — plan; to intend — *打(do) + 算(calculate) — literally "calculating what to do."*
  → same sentence as 周末.

- **啊** (a) — part. — (used at the end of a sentence to indicate confirmation or defense) — *A simple trailing sound added for emphasis.*
  → 是啊，我已经找好饭馆了。— Shì a, wǒ yǐjīng zhǎohǎo fànguǎn le. — Yes, I've already found a restaurant.

- **跟** (gēn) — prep. — with — *足(foot) radical — "following along" with someone.*
  → 我还没想好要不要跟你去呢。— Wǒ hái méi xiǎnghǎo yào bu yào gēn nǐ qù ne. — I haven't decided yet whether to go with you.

- **一直** (yìzhí) — adv. — all the time, continuously — *一(one) + 直(straight) — going straight through, without stopping.*
  → 你一直玩儿电脑游戏。— Nǐ yìzhí wánr diànnǎo yóuxì. — You've been playing computer games the whole time.

- **游戏** (yóuxì) — n. — game — *游(play/roam) + 戏(drama/play) — playful entertainment.*
  → same sentence as 一直.

- **作业** (zuòyè) — n. — homework — *作(do) + 业(task) — the task to be done.*
  → 作业写完了吗？— Zuòyè xiěwán le ma? — Have you finished your homework?

- **着急** (zháojí) — adj. — worried, anxious — *着(attached to) + 急(urgent) — gripped by urgency.*
  → 你怎么一点儿也不着急？— Nǐ zěnme yìdiǎnr yě bù zháojí? — Why aren't you worried at all?

- **复习** (fùxí) — v. — to review — *复(again) + 习(practice) — practicing again.*
  → 我早就复习好了。— Wǒ zǎo jiù fùxí hǎo le. — I already finished reviewing a while ago.

- **南(方)** (nán(fāng)) — n. — south, southern part — *A classic directional character.*
  → 南方啊，我们去年就是这个时候去的。— Nánfāng a, wǒmen qùnián jiù shì zhège shíhou qù de. — The south — we went at this same time last year.

- **北方** (běifāng) — n. — north, northern part — *北 originally pictured two people back-to-back, linked to the cold direction.*
  → 北方好一些，不冷也不热。— Běifāng hǎo yìxiē, bù lěng yě bú rè. — The north is better — not cold, not hot.

- **面包** (miànbāo) — n. — bread — *面(flour) + 包(wrapped bun) — a wrapped flour bun.*
  → 水果、面包、茶都准备好了。— Shuǐguǒ, miànbāo, chá dōu zhǔnbèi hǎo le. — Fruit, bread, and tea are all ready.

- **带** (dài) — v. — to take along, bring — *A belt/sash you'd carry things in, historically.*
  → 我们还带什么？— Wǒmen hái dài shénme? — What else should we bring?

- **地图** (dìtú) — n. — map — *地(land) + 图(picture) — a picture of the land.*
  → 手机、电脑、地图，一个也不能少。— Shǒujī, diànnǎo, dìtú, yí ge yě bù néng shǎo. — Phone, computer, map — not one of them can be missing.

- **搬** (bān) — v. — to move, to carry — *扌(hand) radical — moving something by hand.*
  → 我们是去旅游，不是搬家。— Wǒmen shì qù lǚyóu, bú shì bānjiā. — We're going traveling, not moving house.

## Lesson 2 — 他什么时候回来 (When will he come back)

- **腿** (tuǐ) — n. — leg — *月(flesh/body) radical — a body part.*
  → 我现在腿也疼，脚也疼。— Wǒ xiànzài tuǐ yě téng, jiǎo yě téng. — My legs hurt now, and my feet hurt too.

- **疼** (téng) — adj. — hurt, painful — *疒(sickness) radical — pain as a symptom of illness.*
  → same sentence as 腿.

- **脚** (jiǎo) — n. — foot — *月(flesh) radical — another body part.*
  → same sentence as 腿.

- **树** (shù) — n. — tree — *木(wood) radical — a tree is made of wood.*
  → 那边树多，我们过去坐一下吧。— Nàbian shù duō, wǒmen guòqù zuò yíxià ba. — There are lots of trees over there, let's go sit for a bit.

- **容易** (róngyì) — adj. — easy — *容(allow) + 易(change easily) — something that comes about with ease.*
  → 上山容易下山难。— Shàng shān róngyì xià shān nán. — Going up the mountain is easy, going down is hard.

- **难** (nán) — adj. — difficult — *又(hand) + 隹(bird) historically — catching a bird by hand was hard.*
  → same sentence as 容易.

- **太太** (tàitai) — n. — Mrs., madam — *太(very) doubled — a respectful, elevated form of address.*
  → 喂，你好，请问周明在吗？— Wéi, nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn Zhōu Míng zài ma? — Hello, is Zhou Ming there? *(spoken by 周太太, Mrs. Zhou)*

- **秘书** (mìshū) — n. — secretary — *秘(secret) + 书(document) — one who handles official/confidential documents.*
  → 周经理出去了，不在办公室。— Zhōu jīnglǐ chūqù le, bú zài bàngōngshì. — Manager Zhou has gone out, he's not in the office. *(spoken by the secretary)*

- **经理** (jīnglǐ) — n. — manager — *经(manage) + 理(handle) — one who manages and handles affairs.*
  → same sentence as 秘书.

- **办公室** (bàngōngshì) — n. — office — *办公(conduct business) + 室(room) — the room for conducting business.*
  → same sentence as 秘书.

- **辆** (liàng) — m. — (measure word for vehicles) — *车(vehicle) radical — the standard counter for cars/vehicles.*
  → 我出去叫辆出租车就行了。— Wǒ chūqù jiào liàng chūzūchē jiù xíng le. — I'll just go out and get a taxi.

- **楼** (lóu) — n. — building — *木(wood) radical historically — multi-story buildings were once wooden.*
  → 我上楼去给你拿把伞。— Wǒ shàng lóu qù gěi nǐ ná bǎ sǎn. — I'll go upstairs to get you an umbrella.

- **拿** (ná) — v. — to take, to fetch — *合(combine) + 手(hand) — grasping something with the hand.*
  → same sentence as 楼.

- **把** (bǎ) — m. — (measure word for objects with a handle) — *扌(hand) radical — things held by a handle.*
  → same sentence as 楼.

- **伞** (sǎn) — n. — umbrella — *The character's shape visually resembles an open umbrella.*
  → same sentence as 楼.

- **胖** (pàng) — adj. — fat — *月(flesh) + 半(half) — flesh piling up.*
  → 你看，我这么胖，怎么办呢？— Nǐ kàn, wǒ zhème pàng, zěnme bàn ne? — Look, I'm so fat, what should I do?

- **其实** (qíshí) — adv. — actually — *其(that) + 实(real/true) — the real truth of a matter.*
  → 其实我每天都运动。— Qíshí wǒ měitiān dōu yùndòng. — Actually I exercise every day.

- **瘦** (shòu) — adj. — thin — *疒(sickness) radical — historically associated with unhealthy thinness.*
  → 但是你一点儿也没瘦！— Dànshì nǐ yìdiǎnr yě méi shòu! — But you haven't lost any weight at all!

## Lesson 3 — 桌子上放着很多饮料 (There are plenty of drinks on the table)

- **还是** (háishi) — conj. — or (used in questions) — *还(still) + 是(is) — "is it still this, or that?"*
  → 明天是晴天还是阴天？— Míngtiān shì qíngtiān háishi yīntiān? — Will tomorrow be sunny or cloudy?

- **爬山** (páshān) — v. — to climb a mountain — *爬(climb/crawl) + 山(mountain).*
  → 我们明天要去爬山。— Wǒmen míngtiān yào qù páshān. — We're going hiking tomorrow.

- **小心** (xiǎoxīn) — adj. — careful — *小(small) + 心(heart) — keeping your attention narrow and focused.*
  → 爬山的时候要小心点儿。— Páshān de shíhou yào xiǎoxīn diǎnr. — Be careful when you're climbing.

- **条** (tiáo) — m. — (measure word for pants, dresses, and other long thin things) — *A strip-shaped classifier.*
  → 你觉得这条裤子怎么样？— Nǐ juéde zhè tiáo kùzi zěnmeyàng? — What do you think of these pants?

- **裤子** (kùzi) — n. — pants — *衤(clothing) radical — a garment.*
  → same sentence as 条.

- **记得** (jìde) — v. — to remember — *记(record) + 得(attain) — holding onto a memory.*
  → 我记得你已经有两条这样的裤子了。— Wǒ jìde nǐ yǐjīng yǒu liǎng tiáo zhèyàng de kùzi le. — I remember you already have two pairs like this.

- **衬衫** (chènshān) — n. — shirt — *衤(clothing) radical — another garment word.*
  → 这件衬衫怎么样？— Zhè jiàn chènshān zěnmeyàng? — What about this shirt?

- **元** (yuán) — m. — unit of money (yuan) — *The formal written character for the currency unit.*
  → 这上面写着320元。— Zhè shàngmian xiězhe sānbǎi èrshí yuán. — It says 320 yuan on here.

- **新鲜** (xīnxiān) — adj. — fresh — *新(new) + 鲜(fresh) — newly fresh.*
  → 这些水果真新鲜。— Zhèxiē shuǐguǒ zhēn xīnxiān. — This fruit is really fresh.

- **甜** (tián) — adj. — sweet — *舌(tongue) + 甘(sweet) — sweetness tasted on the tongue.*
  → 西瓜不甜不要钱。— Xīguā bù tián bú yào qián. — If the watermelon isn't sweet, it's free.

- **只** (zhǐ) — adv. — only, solely — *A short, limiting adverb.*
  → 今天晚上只吃水果不吃饭！— Jīntiān wǎnshang zhǐ chī shuǐguǒ bù chī fàn! — Tonight let's only eat fruit, not a proper meal!

- **放** (fàng) — v. — to put, to place — *方(place) + 攵(action) — the action of placing somewhere.*
  → 桌子上放着很多饮料。— Zhuōzi shang fàngzhe hěn duō yǐnliào. — There are lots of drinks placed on the table.

- **饮料** (yǐnliào) — n. — drink, beverage — *饮(drink) + 料(material) — drinkable material.*
  → same sentence as 放.

- **或者** (huòzhě) — conj. — or (used in statements) — *或(or) + 者(that which) — an alternative option.*
  → 茶或者咖啡都可以。— Chá huòzhě kāfēi dōu kěyǐ. — Tea or coffee are both fine.

- **舒服** (shūfu) — adj. — comfortable — *舒(relaxed) + 服(fit) — feeling relaxed and well-suited.*
  → 喝杯热茶会很舒服。— Hē bēi rè chá huì hěn shūfu. — Drinking a cup of hot tea feels very comfortable.

- **花** (huā) — n. — flower — *A classic character for flower.*
  → 花茶、绿茶、红茶，我都喜欢。— Huāchá, lǜchá, hóngchá, wǒ dōu xǐhuan. — Jasmine tea, green tea, black tea — I like them all.

- **绿** (lǜ) — adj. — green — *纟(silk) radical historically — linked to dyed green silk.*
  → same sentence as 花.

## Lesson 4 — 她总是笑着跟客人说话 (She always smiles when talking to customers)

- **比赛** (bǐsài) — n. — match, competition — *比(compare) + 赛(compete) — comparing through competing.*
  → 这是你们比赛的照片吗？— Zhè shì nǐmen bǐsài de zhàopiàn ma? — Is this a photo from your competition?

- **照片** (zhàopiàn) — n. — photo — *照(photograph) + 片(slice/piece) — a captured piece of light.*
  → same sentence as 比赛.

- **年级** (niánjí) — n. — grade (school) — *年(year) + 级(level) — the year-level in school.*
  → 你们都是一个年级的吗？— Nǐmen dōu shì yí ge niánjí de ma? — Are you all in the same grade?

- **又** (yòu) — adv. — and, also (used in the 又…又… pattern) — *A simple connector for listing two qualities together.*
  → 那个又高又漂亮的女孩儿是二年级的。— Nàge yòu gāo yòu piàoliang de nǚháir shì èr niánjí de. — That tall and pretty girl is in second grade.

- **聪明** (cōngming) — adj. — clever, smart — *耳(ear) + related components — sharp of ear and mind.*
  → 她又聪明又热情。— Tā yòu cōngming yòu rèqíng. — She's smart and warm.

- **热情** (rèqíng) — adj. — warm, enthusiastic — *热(hot) + 情(feeling) — warm feelings.*
  → same sentence as 聪明.

- **努力** (nǔlì) — adj. — hard-working — *努(strive) + 力(strength) — striving with strength.*
  → 也很努力。— Yě hěn nǔlì. — and also hardworking.

- **总是** (zǒngshì) — adv. — always — *总(overall) + 是(is) — "it is always the case."*
  → 我看她总是笑着回答老师的问题。— Wǒ kàn tā zǒngshì xiàozhe huídá lǎoshī de wèntí. — I see she always answers the teacher's questions with a smile.

- **回答** (huídá) — v. — to answer — *回(return) + 答(reply) — returning a reply.*
  → same sentence as 总是.

- **站** (zhàn) — v. — to stand — *立(stand) radical — the standing posture.*
  → 那些拿着鲜花站在门口的，都是等她的。— Nàxiē názhe xiānhuā zhàn zài ménkǒu de, dōu shì děng tā de. — Those people standing at the door holding fresh flowers are all waiting for her.

- **饿** (è) — adj. — hungry — *饣(food) radical — a food-related sensation.*
  → 我有点儿饿了。— Wǒ yǒudiǎnr è le. — I'm a bit hungry.

- **超市** (chāoshì) — n. — supermarket — *超(super) + 市(market) — a "super market."*
  → 我们进超市买点儿东西吧。— Wǒmen jìn chāoshì mǎi diǎnr dōngxi ba. — Let's go into the supermarket and buy something.

- **蛋糕** (dàngāo) — n. — cake — *蛋(egg) + 糕(pastry) — an egg-based cake.*
  → 这家超市的蛋糕又便宜又好吃。— Zhè jiā chāoshì de dàngāo yòu piányi yòu hǎochī. — This supermarket's cake is both cheap and tasty.

- **年轻** (niánqīng) — adj. — young — *年(year) + 轻(light) — light in years, i.e. young.*
  → 你们这儿是不是有一个又年轻又漂亮的服务员？— Nǐmen zhèr shì bu shì yǒu yí ge yòu niánqīng yòu piàoliang de fúwùyuán? — Do you have a young and pretty waitress here?

- **认真** (rènzhēn) — adj. — serious, earnest — *认(recognize) + 真(true) — treating things as genuinely important.*
  → 她工作又认真又热情。— Tā gōngzuò yòu rènzhēn yòu rèqíng. — She works both seriously and warmly.

- **客人** (kèrén) — n. — customer, guest — *客(guest) + 人(person).*
  → 她总是笑着跟客人说话。— Tā zǒngshì xiàozhe gēn kèrén shuōhuà. — She always talks to customers with a smile.

## Lesson 5 — 我最近越来越胖了 (I am getting fatter and fatter lately)

- **发烧** (fāshāo) — v. — to have a fever — *发(emit) + 烧(burn) — burning up, i.e. having a fever.*
  → 前几天有点儿发烧，现在好多了。— Qián jǐ tiān yǒudiǎnr fāshāo, xiànzài hǎoduō le. — I had a bit of a fever a few days ago, I'm much better now.

- **为** (wèi) — prep. — for — *A preposition marking who or what something is done for.*
  → 这是我为你买的绿茶。— Zhè shì wǒ wèi nǐ mǎi de lǜchá. — This is green tea I bought for you.

- **照顾** (zhàogù) — v. — to take care of — *照(look after) + 顾(attend to).*
  → 我要在家照顾他。— Wǒ yào zài jiā zhàogù tā. — I need to stay home and take care of him.

- **用** (yòng) — v. — to need, to use — *A basic verb for utility/necessity.*
  → 不用去医院。— Búyòng qù yīyuàn. — No need to go to the hospital.

- **感冒** (gǎnmào) — v. — to catch a cold — *感(feel) + 冒(exposed to) — exposed and feeling unwell.*
  → 昨天吃了感冒药。— Zuótiān chīle gǎnmào yào. — He took cold medicine yesterday.

- **季节** (jìjié) — n. — season — *季(season) + 节(period).*
  → 你最喜欢哪个季节？— Nǐ zuì xǐhuan nǎge jìjié? — Which season do you like best?

- **当然** (dāngrán) — adv. — of course — *当(ought) + 然(so) — "it ought to be so."*
  → 当然是春天。— Dāngrán shì chūntiān. — Spring, of course.

- **春(天)** (chūn(tiān)) — n. — spring — *A character historically depicting the sun warming plants — the season of growth.*
  → same sentence as 当然.

- **草** (cǎo) — n. — grass — *艹(grass) radical doubled.*
  → 草和树都绿了。— Cǎo hé shù dōu lǜ le. — The grass and trees are green.

- **夏(天)** (xià(tiān)) — n. — summer — *Historically linked to a figure dancing in the summer heat.*
  → 我最喜欢夏天。— Wǒ zuì xǐhuan xiàtiān. — I like summer best.

- **裙子** (qúnzi) — n. — dress, skirt — *衤(clothing) radical.*
  → 因为我可以穿漂亮的裙子了。— Yīnwèi wǒ kěyǐ chuān piàoliang de qúnzi le. — because I get to wear pretty dresses.

- **最近** (zuìjìn) — adv. — lately, recently — *最(most) + 近(near) — the nearest/most recent time.*
  → 我最近越来越胖了。— Wǒ zuìjìn yuè lái yuè pàng le. — I've been getting fatter and fatter lately.

- **越** (yuè) — adv. — more, to a greater degree — *Used in the 越来越 ("more and more") pattern.*
  → same sentence as 最近.

## Lesson 6 — 怎么突然找不到了 (Why are they suddenly missing)

- **眼镜** (yǎnjìng) — n. — glasses, spectacles — *目(eye) related roots — a device worn near the eyes.*
  → 我的眼镜呢？— Wǒ de yǎnjìng ne? — Where are my glasses?

- **突然** (tūrán) — adv. — suddenly — *突(sudden) + 然(so) — happening abruptly.*
  → 怎么突然找不到了？— Zěnme tūrán zhǎo bu dào le? — Why can't I suddenly find them?

- **离开** (líkāi) — v. — to leave, to part with — *离(leave) + 开(begin) — starting to be apart from something.*
  → 我离不开眼镜。— Wǒ lí bu kāi yǎnjìng. — I can't be without my glasses.

- **清楚** (qīngchu) — adj. — clear, distinct — *清(clear) + 楚(distinct) — doubled clarity.*
  → 没有眼镜，我一个字也看不清楚。— Méiyǒu yǎnjìng, wǒ yí ge zì yě kàn bu qīngchu. — Without them I can't see a single character clearly.

- **刚才** (gāngcái) — n. — just now — *刚(just) + 才(only just) — a very recent moment.*
  → 是不是刚才放在桌子上了？— Shì bu shì gāngcái fàng zài zhuōzi shang le? — Did you just put them on the table?

- **帮忙** (bāngmáng) — v. — to help — *帮(help) + 忙(busy) — helping with someone's busy tasks.*
  → 你快过来帮忙啊。— Nǐ kuài guòlai bāngmáng a. — Come here quickly and help.

- **特别** (tèbié) — adv. — extraordinarily — *特(special) + 别(distinct) — standing out as special.*
  → 今天这些题特别难。— Jīntiān zhèxiē tí tèbié nán. — Today's questions are especially hard.

- **讲** (jiǎng) — v. — to explain — *讠(speech) radical.*
  → 电话里讲不明白。— Diànhuà li jiǎng bu míngbai. — I can't explain clearly over the phone.

- **明白** (míngbai) — adj. — clear, understood — *明(bright) + 白(clear) — bright and clear understanding.*
  → same sentence as 讲.

- **锻炼** (duànliàn) — v. — to do physical exercise — *锻(forge) + 炼(refine) — forging and refining the body.*
  → 我锻炼完了就过去。— Wǒ duànliàn wán le jiù guòqu. — I'll come over once I finish exercising.

- **音乐** (yīnyuè) — n. — music — *音(sound) + 乐(joy) — joyful sound.*
  → 她喜欢听音乐。— Tā xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè. — She likes listening to music.

- **公园** (gōngyuán) — n. — park — *公(public) + 园(garden).*
  → 那去公园走走。— Nà qù gōngyuán zǒuzou. — Then go for a walk in the park.

- **聊天(儿)** (liáotiān(r)) — v. — to chat — *聊(chat) + 天(day, used idiomatically) — chatting away.*
  → 聊聊天儿吧。— Liáoliao tiānr ba. — and chat.

- **睡着** (shuìzháo) — v. — to fall asleep — *睡(sleep) + 着(achieve, resultative marker) — successfully falling asleep.*

- **有名** (yǒumíng) — adj. — famous — *有(have) + 名(name) — having a well-known name.*
  → same sentence as 不但……而且……

- **同意** (tóngyì) — v. — to agree — *同(same) + 意(intention).*
  → 你家人同意吗？— Nǐ jiārén tóngyì ma? — Does your family agree?

- **相信** (xiāngxìn) — v. — to believe — *相(mutually) + 信(trust).*
  → 我相信他们就会同意的。— Wǒ xiāngxìn tāmen jiù huì tóngyì de. — I believe they'll agree.

- **关于** (guānyú) — prep. — about, regarding — *关(concern) + 于(at).*
  → 关于这个工作，还有什么问题吗？— Guānyú zhège gōngzuò, hái yǒu shénme wèntí ma? — Regarding this job, do you have any other questions?

- **机会** (jīhuì) — n. — opportunity — *机(moment) + 会(occasion) — a fitting occasion.*
  → 谢谢您给我这个机会。— Xièxie nín gěi wǒ zhège jīhuì. — Thank you for giving me this opportunity.

- **国家** (guójiā) — n. — country, nation — *国(nation) + 家(home) — the nation as a home.*
  → 不同的国家有不同的文化。— Bùtóng de guójiā yǒu bùtóng de wénhuà. — Different countries have different cultures.

- **种** (zhǒng) — m. — kind, type — *禾(grain) radical — historically related to seed varieties.*
  → 每种文化都有自己的特点。— Měi zhǒng wénhuà dōu yǒu zìjǐ de tèdiǎn. — Every culture has its own characteristics.

- **特点** (tèdiǎn) — n. — feature — *特(special) + 点(point).*
  → same sentence as 种.

- **奇怪** (qíguài) — adj. — strange, odd — *奇(strange) + 怪(odd) — doubled for strangeness.*
  → 还会觉得有点奇怪。— Hái huì juéde yǒudiǎn qíguài. — You might also find it a bit strange.

- **地** (de) — part. — (connects an adverbial modifier to the verb it modifies) — *A grammatical particle.*
  → 就会慢慢地习惯。— Jiù huì mànman de xíguàn. — You'll gradually get used to it.

## Lesson 19 — 你没看出来吗 (Didn't you recognise him)

- **耳朵** (ěrduo) — n. — ear — *耳(ear) pictograph + 朵(hanging-shape suffix).*
  → 女儿最近喜欢把头发放在耳朵后面。— Nǚ'ér zuìjìn xǐhuan bǎ tóufa fàng zài ěrduo hòumian. — Our daughter has been liking to tuck her hair behind her ears lately.

- **脸** (liǎn) — n. — face — *月(flesh) radical — a body part.*
  → 这样可以使她的脸看上去漂亮一些。— Zhèyàng kěyǐ shǐ tā de liǎn kàn shangqu piàoliang yìxiē. — This makes her face look prettier.

- **短** (duǎn) — adj. — short — *矢(arrow) + 豆(bean) roots — historically linked to shortness.*
  → 她小时候喜欢短头发。— Tā xiǎoshíhou xǐhuan duǎn tóufa. — She used to like short hair when she was little.

- **马** (mǎ) — n. — horse — *A classic pictograph for horse.*
  → 上次我们参加骑马比赛的照片。— Shàng cì wǒmen cānjiā qí mǎ bǐsài de zhàopiàn. — The photos from when we did the horse racing competition last time.

- **张** (zhāng) — m. — (measure word for flat objects like paper, photos) — *弓(bow) + 长(stretch) — a stretched, flat sheet.*
  → 我选了几张洗出来了。— Wǒ xuǎnle jǐ zhāng xǐ chulai le. — I picked a few and had them developed.

- **位** (wèi) — m. — (a respectful measure word for people) — *亻(person) radical — a polite counter for people.*
  → 骑得最快的这位是谁？— Qí de zuì kuài de zhè wèi shì shéi? — Who's this person who rode the fastest?

- **蓝** (lán) — adj. — blue — *艹(grass) radical — historically linked to an indigo dye plant.*
  → 他今天穿蓝西服。— Tā jīntiān chuān lán xīfú. — Today he's wearing a blue suit.

- **秋(天)** (qiū(tiān)) — n. — autumn, fall — *禾(grain) + 火(fire) — the harvest season.*
  → 她去年秋天出生的。— Tā qùnián qiūtiān chūshēng de. — She was born last autumn.

- **过** (guò) — v. — to spend, to pass (time) — *辶(walk) radical — time passing by.*
  → 刚过完一岁生日。— Gāng guòwán yí suì shēngrì. — She just had her first birthday.

- **鸟** (niǎo) — n. — bird — *A pictograph-derived character for bird.*
  → 她就喜欢听她爸爸学鸟叫。— Tā jiù xǐhuan tīng tā bàba xué niǎo jiào. — She just likes listening to her dad imitate bird calls.

- **哭** (kū) — v. — to cry — *口(mouth) doubled + 犬(dog) roots — historically depicting wailing.*
  → 哭的时候。— Kū de shíhou. — When she cries.

- **黄河** (Huáng Hé) — n. — Yellow River — *黄(yellow) + 河(river) — named for its yellow silt.*
  → 我先去看了黄河。— Wǒ xiān qù kànle Huáng Hé. — First I went to see the Yellow River.

- **船** (chuán) — n. — boat, ship — *舟(boat) radical.*
  → 我坐在船上。— Wǒ zuò zài chuán shang. — I sat on a boat.

- **经过** (jīngguò) — v. — to pass by — *经(pass through) + 过(pass) — doubled for passing through.*
  → 一路上经过了很多地方。— Yí lù shang jīngguòle hěn duō dìfang. — Along the way I passed through many places.

## Lesson 20 — 我被他影响了 (I've been influenced by him)

- **照相机** (zhàoxiàngjī) — n. — camera — *照相(take a photo) + 机(machine).*
  → 我的照相机被谁拿走了？— Wǒ de zhàoxiàngjī bèi shéi názǒu le? — Who took my camera?

- **被** (bèi) — prep. — (used to indicate the passive voice) — *衤(clothing) radical historically — "covered by" an action.*
  → same sentence as 照相机.

- **难过** (nánguò) — adj. — sad — *难(hard) + 过(pass through) — hard to get through.*
  → 别难过。— Bié nánguò. — Don't be sad.

- **东** (dōng) — n. — east — *A classic directional character.*
  → 公司东门外不就有一个大商场吗？— Gōngsī dōng mén wài bú jiù yǒu yí ge dà shāngchǎng ma? — Isn't there a big mall right outside the east gate of the company?

- **信用卡** (xìnyòngkǎ) — n. — credit card — *信用(credit/trust) + 卡(card).*
  → 我这个月信用卡里的钱已经花得差不多了。— Wǒ zhège yuè xìnyòngkǎ li de qián yǐjīng huā de chàbuduō le. — I've already spent almost all the money on my credit card this month.

- **关心** (guānxīn) — v. — to care for, to be interested in — *关(concern) + 心(heart).*
  → 你怎么突然关心起体育来了？— Nǐ zěnme tūrán guānxīn qǐ tǐyù lái le? — Why have you suddenly become interested in sports?

- **只有……才……** (zhǐyǒu……cái……) — conj. — only... (that/can)... — *A classic conditional pair.*
  → 只有爱，才能让人有变化。— Zhǐyǒu ài, cái néng ràng rén yǒu biànhuà. — Only love can make a person change.

- **成绩** (chéngjì) — n. — grade, performance, achievement — *成(accomplish) + 绩(achievement).*
  → 我的成绩差极了。— Wǒ de chéngjì chà jí le. — My grades are terrible.

- **碗** (wǎn) — n. — bowl — *A vessel radical — a container for food.*
  → 那个拿着碗吃饭的人是你哥哥吗？— Nàge názhe wǎn chī fàn de rén shì nǐ gēge ma? — Is that person eating with a bowl your brother?

- **分** (fēn) — v. — to distinguish — *八(divide) + 刀(knife) — dividing/separating things.*
  → 只有你们的爸爸妈妈才能分出来哪个是哥哥。— Zhǐyǒu nǐmen de bàba māma cái néng fēn chulai nǎge shì gēge. — Only your parents can tell which one is the older brother.

- **解决** (jiějué) — v. — to solve — *解(untangle) + 决(decide) — untangling to reach a decision.*
  → 不同的问题有不同的解决办法。— Bùtóng de wèntí yǒu bùtóng de jiějué bànfǎ. — Different problems have different solutions.

- **试** (shì) — v. — to try — *讠(speech) radical — historically linked to testing/examining.*
  → 所以得试着做做。— Suǒyǐ děi shìzhe zuòzuo. — So you should try doing them.

- **真正** (zhēnzhèng) — adv. — really, truly — *真(true) + 正(correct) — genuinely and correctly so.*
  → 只有真正做事情的时候，才能了解有多难。— Zhǐyǒu zhēnzhèng zuò shìqing de shíhou, cái néng liǎojiě yǒu duō nán. — Only when you actually do the thing can you understand how difficult it really is.

- **多么** (duōme) — adv. — very, to a great extent — *多(much) + 么(particle) — an intensifying phrase.*
  → 多么难的问题，都会被解决的。— Duōme nán de wèntí, dōu huì bèi jiějué de. — However difficult a problem is, it can always be solved.
