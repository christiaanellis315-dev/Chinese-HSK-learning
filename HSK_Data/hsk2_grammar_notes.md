## Lesson 1 — 九月去北京旅游最好

### 1. 助动词"要" — The Auxiliary Verb "要"

**Chinese:** 用在动词前，表示有做某件事情的愿望。否定形式一般为"不想"。

**English:** Used before a verb, it indicates the desire to do something. Its negative form is usually "不想".

**Examples:**
1. 小王要去，我不想去。(Xiǎo Wáng yào qù, wǒ bù xiǎng qù.) — Xiao Wang wants to go, I don't want to go.
2. A: 你要吃米饭吗？ B: 我不想吃米饭。(Nǐ yào chī mǐfàn ma? Wǒ bù xiǎng chī mǐfàn.) — Do you want to eat rice? I don't want to eat rice.
3. A: 我要去商店买椅子，你去吗？ B: 我不去，我不想买椅子。(Wǒ yào qù shāngdiàn mǎi yǐzi, nǐ qù ma? Wǒ bú qù, wǒ bù xiǎng mǎi yǐzi.) — I want to go to the store to buy a chair, are you going? I'm not going, I don't want to buy a chair.

### 2. 程度副词"最" — The Adverb of Degree "最"

**Chinese:** 表示在同类事物中或某方面占第一位。

**English:** It means being the first among things of the same kind or in a certain aspect.

**Examples:**
1. 大卫的汉语最好。(Dàwèi de Hànyǔ zuì hǎo.) — David's Chinese is the best.
2. 我最喜欢吃米饭。(Wǒ zuì xǐhuan chī mǐfàn.) — I like eating rice the most.
3. 它的眼睛最漂亮。(Tā de yǎnjing zuì piàoliang.) — Its eyes are the most beautiful.

### 3. 概数的表达："几"、"多" — "几" and "多": expressions of approximate numbers

**Chinese:** "几"可以表示10以内的不定个数，后边要有量词。"几"也可以用在"十"之后，表示大于10小于20的数字；也可以用在"十"之前，表示大于20小于100的数字。

**English:** "几" can indicate an indefinite number less than 10, followed by a measure word. When "几" is used after "十", it indicates a number greater than 10 but less than 20; when used before "十", it indicates a number greater than 20 while less than 100.

**Examples:**
1. 车上有几个人。(Chē shang yǒu jǐ ge rén.) — There are a few people in the car.
2. 我想买几本书。(Wǒ xiǎng mǎi jǐ běn shū.) — I want to buy a few books.
3. 我们要不要买几个新的椅子？(Wǒmen yào bu yào mǎi jǐ ge xīn de yǐzi?) — Should we buy a few new chairs?

**Chinese:** "多"与数量词搭配使用。数词是10以下的数字时，"多"用在量词之后；数词是10以上的整数时，"多"用在量词前，这种情况下，"多"和"几"通用。

**English:** "多" can be used together with numeral-measure word phrases. When the numeral is less than 10, "多" should be put behind the measure word. When the numeral is an integer greater than 10, "多" is put before the measure word — in this case "多" and "几" are interchangeable.

## Lesson 2 — 我每天六点起床

### 1. 用"是不是"的问句 — Questions Using "是不是"

**Chinese:** 如果提问的人对某个事实或者情况有比较肯定的估计，为了进一步得到证实，就可以用这种疑问句提问。"是不是"一般用在谓语前面，也可以用在句首或者句尾。

**English:** If one raises a question and is somehow certain about a fact or situation, they can use this kind of question to confirm their guess. "是不是" is usually used before the predicate or at the beginning or end of a sentence.

**Examples:**
1. 你很少生病，是不是喜欢运动？(Nǐ hěn shǎo shēngbìng, shì bu shì xǐhuan yùndòng?) — You rarely get sick, do you like exercising?
2. 是不是明天爸爸休息？(Shì bu shì míngtiān bàba xiūxi?) — Is dad off tomorrow?
3. 我们星期一去北京，是不是？(Wǒmen xīngqīyī qù Běijīng, shì bu shì?) — We're going to Beijing on Monday, right?

### 2. 代词"每" — The Pronoun "每"

**Chinese:** "每"的后边是量词，指全体中的任何一个或一组。比如：每天、每年、每个月、每个星期。

**English:** "每" is used before a measure word, indicating each or every individual or group in the whole, for example "每天" (every day), "每年" (every year), "每个月" (every month) and "每个星期" (every week).

**Examples:**
1. 山姆每年都去中国旅游。(Shānmǔ měi nián dōu qù Zhōngguó lǚyóu.) — Sam travels to China every year.
2. 你每个星期六都工作吗？(Nǐ měi ge xīngqīliù dōu gōngzuò ma?) — Do you work every Saturday?
3. 我每天六点起床。(Wǒ měitiān liù diǎn qǐchuáng.) — I get up at six every day.

### 3. 疑问代词"多" — The Interrogative Pronoun "多"

**Chinese:** 疑问代词"多"用在形容词的前面，对程度进行提问，回答时要说出数量。

**English:** The interrogative pronoun "多" is used before an adjective, asking about the degree of something. A specific quantity should be given to answer the question.

**Examples:**
1. A: 你多大？ B: 我16岁。(Nǐ duō dà? Wǒ shíliù suì.) — How old are you? I'm 16.
2. A: 王医生的儿子多高？ B: 他儿子一米七。(Wáng yīshēng de érzi duō gāo? Tā érzi yì mǐ qī.) — How tall is Dr. Wang's son? His son is 1.7 meters.
3. A: 他多高？ B: 一米八几。(Tā duō gāo? Yì mǐ bā jǐ.) — How tall is he? Over 1.8 meters.

## Lesson 3 — 左边那个红色的是我的

### 1. "的"字短语 — The "的" Phrase

**Chinese:** 代词、形容词、动词等跟"的"组成一个短语，相当于省略了中心语的名词短语。

**English:** "的" can be used after a pronoun, an adjective or a verb to form a phrase which is equivalent to a nominal phrase with its headword omitted.

**Examples:**
1. 这本书不是我的。（=我的书）(Zhè běn shū bú shì wǒ de.) — This book isn't mine.
2. 这个杯子是昨天买的。（=昨天买的杯子）(Zhège bēizi shì zuótiān mǎi de.) — This cup was bought yesterday.
3. 这块手表是你的吗？（=你的手表）(Zhè kuài shǒubiǎo shì nǐ de ma?) — Is this watch yours?

### 2. 一下 — The Numeral Classifier "一下"

**Chinese:** "一下"用在动词后面，表示一次短暂的动作，相当于动词的重叠式AA（见第8课），宾语可以省略。

**English:** "一下" is used after a verb to indicate a short action, similar to the reduplicative form (AA) of a verb (see Lesson 8). The object of the verb can be omitted.

### 3. 语气副词"真" — The Modal Adverb "真"

**Chinese:** "真+形容词"表示感叹的语气，意思是的确、实在。

**English:** The structure "真 + adjective" expresses an exclamatory mood, meaning "really, indeed".

**Examples:**
1. 你真好！(Nǐ zhēn hǎo!) — You're so nice!
2. 今天天气真好！(Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo!) — The weather is really nice today!
3. 你女儿的房间真漂亮！(Nǐ nǚ'ér de fángjiān zhēn piàoliang!) — Your daughter's room is really pretty!

## Lesson 4 — 这个工作是他帮我介绍的

### 1. "是……的"句：强调施事 — The Structure "是……的": emphasizing the agent of an action

**Chinese:** 在已经知道事情发生的情况下，可以用"是……的"强调动作的发出者。否定形式在"是"的前边加"不"。

**English:** When the occurrence of an action is known, "是……的" can be used to emphasize the agent of the action. In the negative form, "不" is added before "是".

### 2. 表示时间：……的时候 — "……的时候" Indicating Time

**Chinese:** "数量+的时候"表示时间。"动词+的时候"也表示时间。

**English:** "Num-M + 的时候" indicates time. "V + 的时候" also indicates time.

**Examples:**
1. 今天早上八点的时候我没在家。(Jīntiān zǎoshang bā diǎn de shíhou wǒ méi zài jiā.) — I wasn't home at eight this morning.
2. 我十八岁的时候一个人来到北京。(Wǒ shíbā suì de shíhou yí ge rén láidào Běijīng.) — I came to Beijing alone when I was 18.
3. 我十一岁的时候开始踢足球。(Wǒ shíyī suì de shíhou kāishǐ tī zúqiú.) — I started playing football when I was 11.
4. 我睡觉的时候，我妈妈在做饭。(Wǒ shuìjiào de shíhou, wǒ māma zài zuò fàn.) — When I was sleeping, my mom was cooking.
5. 麦克到学校的时候下雨了。(Màikè dào xuéxiào de shíhou xià yǔ le.) — It started raining when Mike arrived at school.
6. 王老师工作的时候，她丈夫开车去医院了。(Wáng lǎoshī gōngzuò de shíhou, tā zhàngfu kāichē qù yīyuàn le.) — While Teacher Wang was working, her husband drove to the hospital.

### 3. 时间副词"已经" — The Adverb of Time "已经"

**Chinese:** "已经"表示动作完成或者达到某种程度。

**English:** "已经" indicates that an action has been completed or having reached a certain degree.

**Examples:**
1. 王老师已经回家了。(Wáng lǎoshī yǐjīng huí jiā le.) — Teacher Wang has already gone home.
2. 我的身体已经好了。(Wǒ de shēntǐ yǐjīng hǎo le.) — I've already recovered.
3. （足球我）已经踢了十年了。(Zúqiú wǒ yǐjīng tīle shí nián le.) — I've already been playing football for ten years.

## Lesson 5 — 就买这件吧

### 1. 副词"就" — The Adverb "就"

**Chinese:** "就+动词"表示承接上文，得出结论。

**English:** The structure "就 + verb" indicates a conclusion or a resolution made on the basis of what's been mentioned previously.

**Examples:**
1. 你不想去，就在家休息吧。(Nǐ bù xiǎng qù, jiù zài jiā xiūxi ba.) — If you don't want to go, just rest at home.
2. 这儿的咖啡不错，就喝咖啡吧。(Zhèr de kāfēi búcuò, jiù hē kāfēi ba.) — The coffee here is good, let's just have coffee.
3. 就做你爱吃的鱼吧。(Jiù zuò nǐ ài chī de yú ba.) — I'll just make the fish you like.

**Note:** 在"颜色还可以，就是有点儿大"这句话中，"就是"表示让步。 / In the sentence "颜色还可以，就是有点儿大", "就是" indicates concession.

### 2. 语气副词"还" (1) — The Modal Adverb "还" (1)

**Chinese:** "还+形容词"表示勉强过得去。

**English:** The structure "还 + Adj" means that something is passable or acceptable.

**Examples:**
1. A: 你身体怎么样？ B: 还好。(Nǐ shēntǐ zěnmeyàng? Hái hǎo.) — How's your health? Not bad.
2. A: 这件衣服大吗？ B: 还行，不太大。(Zhè jiàn yīfu dà ma? Hái xíng, bú tài dà.) — Is this garment big? It's OK, not too big.
3. A: 昨天的考试怎么样？ B: 我觉得听和说还可以，读和写不好。(Zuótiān de kǎoshì zěnmeyàng? Wǒ juéde tīng hé shuō hái kěyǐ, dú hé xiě bù hǎo.) — How was yesterday's exam? Listening and speaking were OK, reading and writing weren't good.

### 3. 程度副词"有点儿" — The Adverbial Modifier "有点儿"

**Chinese:** "有点儿+形容词/动词"，一般表示说话人消极、不满的情绪。

**English:** The structure "有点儿 + adjective/verb" indicates the speaker's negative mood or complaint.

**Examples:**
1. 今天天气有点儿冷。(Jīntiān tiānqì yǒudiǎnr lěng.) — The weather is a bit cold today.
2. 我昨天有点儿累。(Wǒ zuótiān yǒudiǎnr lèi.) — I was a bit tired yesterday.
3. （这件衣服）有点儿大。(Zhè jiàn yīfu yǒudiǎnr dà.) — This garment is a bit big.

## Lesson 6 — 你怎么不吃了

### 1. 疑问代词"怎么" — The Interrogative Pronoun "怎么"

**Chinese:** 用"怎么+动词/形容词"询问事情的原因，多表示奇怪、惊讶的语气。

**English:** The structure "怎么 + verb/adjective" is used to ask about the reason for something, indicating surprise or astonishment.

### 2. 量词的重叠 — Reduplication of Measure Words

**Chinese:** 量词重叠后表示"每一"的意思，常用来强调在某个范围内的每个成员都具有某种特征，后面一般用"都"。

**English:** When a measure word is reduplicated, it means "every/each", emphasizing that a specific feature is shared by every member in a certain group, usually followed by "都".

### 3. 关联词"因为……，所以……" — The Pair of Conjunctions "因为……，所以……"

**Chinese:** 连接两个表示因果关系的分句，前一分句表示原因，后一分句表示结果。使用时可以成对出现，也可以省略其中一个。

**English:** The two conjunctions are used to connect two clauses in a causative relation, the first clause being the cause and the second being the effect. One can use both or either of them in a sentence.

**Examples:**
1. 因为她生病了，所以没去学校。(Yīnwèi tā shēngbìng le, suǒyǐ méi qù xuéxiào.) — Because she got sick, she didn't go to school.
2. 因为他每天跑步，所以身体很好。(Yīnwèi tā měitiān pǎobù, suǒyǐ shēntǐ hěn hǎo.) — Because he jogs every day, he's very healthy.
3. 因为昨天下雨，所以我们都没去（打篮球）。(Yīnwèi zuótiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen dōu méi qù dǎ lánqiú.) — Because it rained yesterday, none of us went to play basketball.

## Lesson 7 — 你家离公司远吗

### 1. 语气副词"还" (2) — The Modal Adverb "还" (2)

**Chinese:** 表示动作或状态的延续，否定式用"还没"。

**English:** It indicates the continuation of an action or a state. Its negative form is "还没".

**Examples:**
1. 八点了，他还在睡觉。(Bā diǎn le, tā hái zài shuìjiào.) — It's eight already, he's still sleeping.
2. 你怎么还没吃饭？(Nǐ zěnme hái méi chī fàn?) — Why haven't you eaten yet?
3. 他还在教室学习呢。(Tā hái zài jiàoshì xuéxí ne.) — He's still studying in the classroom.

### 2. 时间副词"就" — The Adverb of Time "就"

**Chinese:** 强调说话人认为事情发生得早，进行得快、顺利。

**English:** It is used for emphasis, indicating that the speaker thinks something happened early or went fast and well.

**Examples:**
1. 同学们七点半就来教室了。(Tóngxuémen qī diǎn bàn jiù lái jiàoshì le.) — The classmates arrived at the classroom as early as 7:30.
2. 我坐飞机一个半小时就到北京了。(Wǒ zuò fēijī yí ge bàn xiǎoshí jiù dào Běijīng le.) — It only took me an hour and a half by plane to get to Beijing.
3. （我）二十分钟就到。(Wǒ èrshí fēnzhōng jiù dào.) — I'll be there in just 20 minutes.

### 3. 离 — The Verb "离"

**Chinese:** 用来表示处所、时间、目的的距离。

**English:** It indicates the distance from a place, moment or purpose.

**Examples:**
1. 我家离学校很远。(Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn yuǎn.) — My home is far from school.
2. 学校离机场有20多公里。(Xuéxiào lí jīchǎng yǒu èrshí duō gōnglǐ.) — The school is more than 20 kilometers from the airport.
3. 离我的生日还有一个多星期呢！(Lí wǒ de shēngrì hái yǒu yí ge duō xīngqī ne!) — It's still more than a week until my birthday!

### 4. 语气助词"呢" — The Modal Particle "呢"

**Chinese:** 用于陈述句尾，可用在形容词谓语句和动词谓语句后边，表示确认事实，使对方信服，含有夸张的语气。

**English:** It is used at the end of a declarative sentence or after a sentence with an adjectival or verbal predicate to confirm a fact and convince someone in an exaggerative mood.

**Examples:**
1. 八点上课，时间还早呢。(Bā diǎn shàngkè, shíjiān hái zǎo ne.) — Class starts at eight, there's still plenty of time.
2. 医院离我们这儿还远呢。(Yīyuàn lí wǒmen zhèr hái yuǎn ne.) — The hospital is quite far from here.
3. 坐公共汽车要一个多小时呢！(Zuò gōnggòng qìchē yào yí ge duō xiǎoshí ne!) — It takes more than an hour by bus!

## Lesson 8 — 让我想想再告诉你

### 1. 疑问句"……，好吗" — The Interrogative Sentence "……，好吗"

**Chinese:** 常用来表示询问别人的意见和看法。

**English:** It is used to ask about another person's idea or opinion.

**Examples:**
1. 我们一起去吃饭，好吗？(Wǒmen yìqǐ qù chī fàn, hǎo ma?) — Let's go eat together, OK?
2. 你明天下午给我打电话，好吗？(Nǐ míngtiān xiàwǔ gěi wǒ dǎ diànhuà, hǎo ma?) — Call me tomorrow afternoon, OK?
3. 我们下午去看电影，好吗？(Wǒmen xiàwǔ qù kàn diànyǐng, hǎo ma?) — Let's go see a movie this afternoon, OK?

### 2. 副词"再" — The Adverb "再"

**Chinese:** 表示一个动作或一种状态重复或继续，也可以用来表示一个动作将要在某一情况下出现。

**English:** It indicates the repetition or continuation of an action or a state. It can also indicate that an action will happen under a certain circumstance.

### 3. 兼语句 — Pivotal Sentences

**Chinese:** 兼语句的谓语是由两个动词短语组成，前一个动词的宾语是第二个动词的主语。前一个动词常常是"请、让、叫"等词语。

**English:** The predicate of a pivotal sentence is made up of two verbal phrases, the object of the first verb being the subject of the second. The first verb is often a causative verb, such as "请" (to invite), "让" (to let) and "叫" (to ask).

### 4. 动词的重叠 — Reduplication of Verbs

**Chinese:** 动词的重叠形式用来表达短时间、少量、轻微、尝试的意思，语气比较轻松、随便，多用于口语中。

**English:** The reduplicative form of a verb indicates a short time, a small quantity, a slight degree or an attempt, conveying a relaxed and casual mood. It is often used in spoken Chinese.

**Patterns:** Monosyllabic verbs: A → AA → A一A (说 → 说说 → 说一说). Disyllabic verbs: AB → ABAB (学习 → 学习学习).

## Lesson 9 — 题太多，我没做完

### 1. 结果补语 — Complements of Result

**Chinese:** 一些动词或形容词可以放在动词后边，补充、说明动作的结果，它们叫作结果补语。在结果补语前加"没（有）"表示否定，句尾不能用"了"。表示疑问时，常在句尾加上"（了）没有"。

**English:** Some verbs or adjectives can be used after a verb to add remarks about the result of an action. They are called complements of result. "没（有）" is added before the verb to form the negative form, in which case "了" cannot appear at the end of the sentence. To form a question, "（了）没有" is often added at the end of the sentence.

### 2. 介词"从" — The Preposition "从"

**Chinese:** 介词"从"引出一段时间、一段路程、一件事情的经过或者一个序列的起点，后面常跟"到"一起搭配使用。

**English:** The preposition "从" introduces the starting point of a period of time, a distance, a process or a sequence, often used together with "到".

### 3. "第~"表示顺序 — "第~" Indicating Order

**Chinese:** "第"常被放在数量短语前边，表示顺序。

**English:** "第" is often used before a numeral-measure-word phrase to indicate order.

**English:** This sentence structure is used to dissuade or forbid somebody from doing something.

**Chinese:** 介词"对"可以表示人和人、人和事物、事物和事物之间的对待关系。

**English:** The preposition "对" can indicate a subject-target relation between people or things.

## Lesson 11 — 他比我大三岁

### 1. 动词结构做定语 — A Verb (Phrase) Used as an Attributive Modifier

**Chinese:** 动词或动词短语做定语时，定语和中心语之间要加"的"。

**English:** When a verb or verb phrase is used as an attributive modifier, "的" should be put between the modifier and the word modified.

### 2. "比"字句 (1) — The "比" Sentence (1)

**Chinese:** 用"比"表示比较的句子叫"比"字句。"比"字句的谓语可以是形容词。否定形式可以用"A没有B……"表示。

**English:** A "比" sentence is a sentence using "比" to make a comparison. The predicate of the sentence can be an adjective. "A没有B……" is the negative form of a "比" sentence.

要表达事物之间的差别时，用具体数量表示具体差别，用"一点儿""一些"表示差别不大，用"多""得多"表示差别大。

When describing the difference between things, a specific number is used to specify the difference, "一点儿" or "一些" indicates the difference is slight, and "多" or "得多" indicates the difference is significant.

### 3. 助动词"可能" — The Auxiliary Verb "可能"

**Chinese:** "可能"表示估计、也许、或许。常用在动词前，也可用在主语前。

**English:** It means "maybe" indicating an estimation. It can be used before the verb or subject of a sentence.

**Examples:**
1. 他可能早就知道这件事情了。(Tā kěnéng zǎo jiù zhīdào zhè jiàn shìqing le.) — He probably already knew about this a while ago.
2. 可能我明天不来上课了。(Kěnéng wǒ míngtiān bù lái shàngkè le.) — I might not come to class tomorrow.
3. 你可能不认识她。(Nǐ kěnéng bú rènshi tā.) — You might not know her.

## Lesson 12 — 你穿得太少了

### 1. 程度补语 — Complements of Degree

**Chinese:** 程度补语是用来表示程度的，在形式上常用结构助词"得"连接程度补语，补充、说明动作进行的程度或事物性质、状态所达到的程度。也有不用"得"连接的情况。如果有宾语时，要把宾语提前，或者重复动词。表示否定时，要把否定词放在结构助词"得"的后边。程度补语的疑问形式是在结构助词"得"的后面使用"Adj+不+Adj"，构成正反疑问句。

**English:** A complement of degree describes the degree of something. The structural particle "得" is often used to introduce a complement of degree which makes remarks on the extent or degree an action or the quality or state of something has reached. Sometimes "得" is not used. If the verb takes an object, the object should be put before the verb, or the verb be reduplicated. In the negative form, the negative word should be put after the structural particle "得". In the interrogative form, "得" is followed by the structure "Adj + 不 + Adj", forming an affirmative-negative sentence.

### 2. "比"字句 (2) — The "比" Sentence (2)

**Chinese:** 如果动词带程度补语，"比"可以放在动词前，也可以放在补语前。

**English:** If a verb takes a complement of degree, "比" can be put before the verb or the complement.

## Lesson 13 — 门开着呢

### 1. 结构助词"着" — The Structural Particle "着"

**Chinese:** 动词加上结构助词"着"可以表示某种状态的持续。在动词前加"没"表示否定。在句末加"没有"表示疑问。

**English:** A verb followed by the structural particle "着" can indicate the continuation of a certain state. In the negative form, "没" is added before the verb. In the interrogative form, "没有" is added at the end of the sentence.

### 2. 反问句"不是……吗" — The Rhetorical Question "不是……吗"

**Chinese:** "不是……吗"常用来表示提醒或者表达说话人的不理解、不满等。

**English:** "不是……吗" is often used to remind someone of something or to show confusion or dissatisfaction.

**Examples:**
1. 不是说今天有雨吗？怎么没下？(Bú shì shuō jīntiān yǒu yǔ ma? Zěnme méi xià?) — Didn't they say it would rain today? Why hasn't it rained?
2. 你不是北京人吗？怎么不会说北京话？(Nǐ bú shì Běijīngrén ma? Zěnme bú huì shuō Běijīnghuà?) — Aren't you from Beijing? Why can't you speak the Beijing dialect?
3. （她是你的女朋友？）她不是有男朋友了吗？(Tā shì nǐ de nǚpéngyou? Tā bú shì yǒu nánpéngyou le ma?) — (She's your girlfriend?) Doesn't she already have a boyfriend?

### 3. 介词"往" — The Preposition "往"

**Chinese:** 介词"往"常常用来指示方向。

**English:** The preposition "往" is often used to indicate direction.

**Examples:**
1. 从这儿往前走，就是我们学校。(Cóng zhèr wǎng qián zǒu, jiù shì wǒmen xuéxiào.) — Walk straight ahead from here, and that's our school.
2. 你看，往左走是医院，往右走是银行。(Nǐ kàn, wǎng zuǒ zǒu shì yīyuàn, wǎng yòu zǒu shì yínháng.) — Look, go left for the hospital, go right for the bank.
3. 从这儿一直往前走，到了前面的路口再往右走。(Cóng zhèr yìzhí wǎng qián zǒu, dàole qiánmiàn de lùkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu.) — Walk straight ahead from here, and turn right at the crossing ahead.

## Lesson 14 — 你看过那个电影吗

### 1. 结构助词"过" — The Structural Particle "过"

**Chinese:** 动词后加结构助词"过"，一般用来表示过去有过的经历，这些动作行为没有持续到现在。在动词前边加"没（有）"表示否定。在句末加"没有"表示疑问。

**English:** A verb followed by the structural particle "过" usually indicates a past experience action which hasn't lasted to the present. In the negative form, "没（有）" is added before the verb. In the interrogative form, "没有" is added at the end of the sentence.

### 2. 关联词"虽然……，但是……" — The Pair of Conjunctions "虽然……，但是……"

**Chinese:** "虽然……，但是……"连接两个分句，构成一种转折关系。

**English:** The conjunctions "虽然……，但是……" connect two clauses, forming a complex sentence indicating an adversative relation.

**Examples:**
1. 虽然外面很冷，但是房间里很热。(Suīrán wàimian hěn lěng, dànshì fángjiān li hěn rè.) — Although it's cold outside, the room is very warm.
2. 虽然汉字很难，但是我很喜欢写汉字。(Suīrán Hànzì hěn nán, dànshì wǒ hěn xǐhuan xiě Hànzì.) — Although Chinese characters are difficult, I really like writing them.
3. 虽然是晴天，但是很冷。(Suīrán shì qíngtiān, dànshì hěn lěng.) — Although it's sunny, it's very cold.

### 3. 动量补语"次" — The Complement of Frequency "次"

**Chinese:** 动量补语"次"常放在谓语动词的后边，用来表示动作发生、进行的次数。宾语是表示地点的名词时，动量补语可以放在宾语前，也可以放在宾语后。

**English:** The complement of frequency "次" is usually used after the predicate verb, indicating the number of times that an action has taken place. When the object of a verb is a place, the complement of frequency can be put either before or after the object.

## Lesson 15 — 新年就要到了

### 1. 动作的状态："要……了" — "要……了" Indicating the State of an Action

**Chinese:** 用"快要/快/就要/要……了"表示某事将要发生。如果句子中有时间状语，只能用"就要……了"。

**English:** The structure "快要/快/就要/要……了" indicates that something is going to happen. If there is an adverbial of time in the sentence, then only "就要……了" can be used.

### 2. "都……了" — The Structure "都……了"

**Chinese:** "都……了"可以表示"已经"的意思，通常含有强调或不满的语气。

**English:** The structure "都……了" means "already", usually conveying an emphatic or a complaining tone.

**Examples:**
1. 都8点了，快点儿起床吧。(Dōu bā diǎn le, kuài diǎnr qǐchuáng ba.) — It's already eight, hurry up and get out of bed.
2. 你都十岁了，可以自己洗衣服了。(Nǐ dōu shí suì le, kěyǐ zìjǐ xǐ yīfu le.) — You're already ten, you can wash your own clothes now.
3. 都等她半个小时了。(Dōu děng tā bàn ge xiǎoshí le.) — We've already been waiting for her for half an hour.
