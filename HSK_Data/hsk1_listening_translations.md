Source: these are the full sentences used in each lesson's Listening (A/B/C) mode,
originally sourced from the HSK1 workbook's Part III listening exercises and cross-
verified against the workbook's answer key. The Chinese/pinyin already exist in the
app's listening data — this file adds the English translation of each full sentence,
for display on the answer-reveal screen (after pinyin, after hanzi).

## Lesson 4

1. 他叫李朋，他是中国人。
   **English:** His name is Li Peng, he is Chinese.

2. 她是中国人，她是我的汉语老师。
   **English:** She is Chinese, she is my Chinese teacher.

3. 她叫安妮，她不是我同学，她是我朋友。
   **English:** Her name is Annie, she is not my classmate, she is my friend.

4. 她叫李心，是我的中国朋友。
   **English:** Her name is Li Xin, she is my Chinese friend.

## Lesson 5

2. 他是我的汉语老师，他今年45岁。
   **English:** He is my Chinese teacher, he is 45 this year.

3. 他是我的中国朋友，他家有3口人。

   **English:** His name is David, he is an American student, he is 22 this year.

## Lesson 6

1. 我会做中国菜，中国菜很好吃。
   **English:** I can cook Chinese food, Chinese food is delicious.

2. 我的汉语名字是李朋，我会写我的汉语名字。
   **English:** My Chinese name is Li Peng, I can write my Chinese name.

3. 我的好朋友是中国人，他会说汉语。
   **English:** My good friend is Chinese, he can speak Chinese.

   **English:** My mom can't write Chinese characters, I can write Chinese characters.

## Lesson 7

1. 明天是3月19号，星期三。
   **English:** Tomorrow is March 19th, Wednesday.

2. 昨天是8月20号。
   **English:** Yesterday was August 20th.

3. 妈妈的生日是1月22号。
   **English:** Mom's birthday is January 22nd.

4. 我朋友会做中国菜，明天我去他家吃中国菜。
   **English:** My friend can cook Chinese food, tomorrow I'm going to his house to eat Chinese food.

## Lesson 8

   **English:** Tomorrow he wants to go to the store to buy a cup.

   **English:** I want to drink tea. What about you?

3. 大卫今天下午去中国朋友家喝茶。
   **English:** David is going to his Chinese friend's house this afternoon to drink tea.

4. 这个菜38块，那个菜49块。
   **English:** This dish is 38 kuai, that dish is 49 kuai.

## Lesson 12
