# HSK2 Vocabulary — Words, Mnemonics & Example Sentences

Source: every word's pinyin, part of speech, and English meaning is taken directly
from the textbook's "New Words" tables per lesson. Every example sentence is the
actual dialogue line that word first appears in, with the textbook's own English
translation — same extraction discipline used for HSK1. Mnemonics are memory aids
written to help retention; they are not textbook content.

169 words total across 15 lessons. HSK2's syllabus is 300 words including HSK1's
150 — so these 169 are the new words this book introduces (slightly more than the
"~150 new" estimate in the book's own guide, since a few multi-character phrases
count as single vocab entries here).

## Lesson 1 — 九月去北京旅游最好 (September is the best time to visit Beijing)

- **旅游** (lǚyóu) — v. — to travel, to take a trip — *Sounds like "lyu-yo" — imagine crying "lyu-yo!" with joy on vacation.*
  → 我要去北京旅游。— Wǒ yào qù Běijīng lǚyóu. — I'm thinking of a trip to Beijing.

- **觉得** (juéde) — v. — to think, to feel — *"Jway-duh" — a "clue" (jué) to how you "feel."*
  → 你觉得什么时候去最好？— Nǐ juéde shénme shíhou qù zuì hǎo? — When do you think is the best time?

- **最** (zuì) — adv. — most, to the greatest extent — *Short punchy word for "the most" — think "zui" = "zenith."*
  → 九月去北京旅游最好。— Jiǔ yuè qù Běijīng lǚyóu zuì hǎo. — September is the best time to visit Beijing.

- **为什么** (wèi shénme) — why — *"Way shummuh" — literally "for what," the standard "why."*
  → 为什么？— Wèi shénme? — Why?

- **也** (yě) — adv. — also, too — *Already known from HSK1 — tack it onto a sentence like "too."*
  → 九月的北京天气不冷也不热。— Jiǔ yuè de Běijīng tiānqì bù lěng yě bú rè. — It's neither cold nor hot in September in Beijing.

- **运动** (yùndòng) — n./v. — sport; to exercise, work out — *"Yun-dong" — imagine a "young dude" working out.*
  → 你喜欢什么运动？— Nǐ xǐhuan shénme yùndòng? — What sport do you like?

- **踢足球** (tī zúqiú) — to play football — *踢(kick) + 足球(foot-ball) — literally "kick football."*
  → 我最喜欢踢足球。— Wǒ zuì xǐhuan tī zúqiú. — I like playing football most.

- **一起** (yìqǐ) — adv. — together — *Already known from HSK1 — "one rise," doing things as one.*
  → 下午我们一起去踢足球吧。— Xiàwǔ wǒmen yìqǐ qù tī zúqiú ba. — Let's go to play football together this afternoon.

- **要** (yào) — aux. — to want to, would like to — *Sounds like "yow" — "yow, I want that!"*
  → 我们要不要买几个新的椅子？— Wǒmen yào bu yào mǎi jǐ ge xīn de yǐzi? — Don't you think we should buy some new chairs?

- **新** (xīn) — adj. — new — *Similar sound to English "shin" — a new pair of shin guards.*
  → 明天下午我们一起去买新的椅子。— (see 要, same dialogue) — new chairs.

- **它** (tā) — pron. — it — *Same sound as 他/她 but for non-people — a neutral "it."*
  → 它叫花花。— Tā jiào Huāhua. — It's called Huahua.

- **眼睛** (yǎnjing) — n. — eye — *眼(eye) + 睛(eyeball) — doubled up for "the organ of sight."*
  → 我觉得它的眼睛最漂亮。— Wǒ juéde tā de yǎnjing zuì piàoliang. — I think its eyes are the most beautiful.

## Lesson 2 — 我每天六点起床 (I get up at six every day)

- **生病** (shēngbìng) — v. — to fall ill, to be sick — *生(occur) + 病(illness) — an illness "occurring."*
  → 你很少生病，是不是喜欢运动？— Nǐ hěn shǎo shēngbìng, shì bu shì xǐhuan yùndòng? — You seldom get sick, don't you like sports?

- **每** (měi) — pron. — every, each — *Sounds like "may" — "it may happen every time."*
  → 我每天早上都要出去跑步。— Wǒ měitiān zǎoshang dōu yào chūqù pǎobù. — I go out for a jog every morning.

- **早上** (zǎoshang) — n. — morning — *早(early) + 上(period) — the early period of the day.*
  → same sentence as 每 above.

- **跑步** (pǎobù) — v. — to run, to jog — *跑(run) + 步(step) — running, step by step.*
  → same sentence as 每 above.

- **起床** (qǐchuáng) — v. — to get up, get out of bed — *起(rise) + 床(bed) — rising from bed.*
  → 你每天几点起床？— Nǐ měitiān jǐ diǎn qǐchuáng? — What time do you get up?

- **药** (yào) — n. — medicine, drug — *Same sound as 要(want) — you "want" your medicine when sick.*
  → 吃药了吗？— Chī yào le ma? — Did you take your medicine?

- **身体** (shēntǐ) — n. — body — *身(body) + 体(form/body) — doubled for emphasis, already seen in HSK1.*
  → 现在身体怎么样？— Xiànzài shēntǐ zěnmeyàng? — How do you feel now?

- **出院** (chūyuàn) — v. — to leave hospital, be discharged — *出(exit) + 院(institution) — exiting the hospital.*
  → 什么时候能出院？— Shénme shíhou néng chūyuàn? — When can you leave the hospital?

- **出** (chū) — v. — to come/go out — *Simple directional verb — "exit."*
  → same root as 出院.

- **高** (gāo) — adj. — tall, high — *Sounds like "gow" — imagine a tall giraffe going "gow!"*
  → 他多高？— Tā duō gāo? — How tall is he?

- **米** (mǐ) — m. — meter — *Also means "rice" — picture a meter-long rice stick as the unit.*
  → 一米八几。— Yì mǐ bā jǐ. — More than 180 centimeters.

- **知道** (zhīdào) — v. — to know — *知(know) + 道(way/path) — knowing the way/facts.*
  → 你怎么知道这么多啊？— Nǐ zěnme zhīdào zhème duō a? — How come you know so much about him?

- **休息** (xiūxi) — v. — to rest — *休(rest) + 息(breathe) — resting and catching your breath.*
  → 张老师星期六也不休息啊？— Zhāng lǎoshī xīngqīliù yě bù xiūxi a? — Doesn't Mr. Zhang take Saturday off?

- **忙** (máng) — adj. — busy — *心(heart) radical — your heart racing because you're busy.*
  → 他这几天很忙。— Tā zhè jǐ tiān hěn máng. — He has been busy lately.

- **时间** (shíjiān) — n. — time — *时(time) + 间(interval) — the interval of time.*
  → 没有时间休息。— Méiyǒu shíjiān xiūxi. — He has no time to rest.

## Lesson 3 — 左边那个红色的是我的 (The red one on the left is mine)

- **手表** (shǒubiǎo) — n. — watch — *手(hand) + 表(surface/dial) — the dial you wear on your hand.*
  → 这块手表是你的吗？— Zhè kuài shǒubiǎo shì nǐ de ma? — Is this watch yours?

- **千** (qiān) — num. — thousand — *Simple character — a single stroke crossing another, like counting a big stack.*
  → 三千多块。— Sānqiān duō kuài. — More than 3,000 yuan.

- **报纸** (bàozhǐ) — n. — newspaper — *报(report) + 纸(paper) — paper that reports news.*
  → 这是今天早上的报纸吗？— Zhè shì jīntiān zǎoshang de bàozhǐ ma? — Is it this morning's newspaper?

- **送** (sòng) — v. — to send, deliver — *辶(walk/movement) radical — moving something to deliver it.*
  → 是不是送报纸的来了？— Shì bu shì sòng bàozhǐ de lái le? — Is that the man who delivers newspaper?

- **一下** (yíxià) — num-m. — (after a verb, a brief/casual attempt) — *一(one) + 下(instance) — "just once," a quick try.*
  → 我看一下。— Wǒ kàn yíxià. — Let me see (take a quick look).

- **牛奶** (niúnǎi) — n. — milk — *牛(cow) + 奶(milk) — milk from a cow.*
  → 是送牛奶的。— Shì sòng niúnǎi de. — It's the milkman.

- **房间** (fángjiān) — n. — room — *房(house) + 间(space between) — a space within a house.*
  → 这是谁的房间？— Zhè shì shéi de fángjiān? — Whose room is this?

- **丈夫** (zhàngfu) — n. — husband — *丈(measure/senior) + 夫(man) — the senior man of the house.*
  → 这是我和我丈夫的。— Zhè shì wǒ hé wǒ zhàngfu de. — It's my husband's and mine.

- **旁边** (pángbiān) — n. — beside, next to — *旁(side) + 边(edge) — the edge/side of something.*
  → 旁边那个小的房间是我女儿的。— Pángbiān nàge xiǎo de fángjiān shì wǒ nǚ'ér de. — The small one beside it is my daughter's.

- **真** (zhēn) — adv. — really, indeed — *Short, emphatic — "真" sounds a bit like "jen-uine," genuinely so.*
  → 你女儿的房间真漂亮！— Nǐ nǚ'ér de fángjiān zhēn piàoliang! — Your daughter's room is so pretty!

- **粉色** (fěnsè) — n. — pink — *粉(powder) + 色(color) — the color of powder, i.e. pink.*
  → 都是粉色的。— Dōu shì fěnsè de. — It's all pink.

- **粉** (fěn) — adj. — pink — same root as 粉色.

- **颜色** (yánsè) — n. — color — *颜(face/countenance) + 色(color) — historically linked to complexion, now general "color."*
  → 粉色是我女儿最喜欢的颜色。— Fěnsè shì wǒ nǚ'ér zuì xǐhuan de yánsè. — Pink is her favorite color.

- **左边** (zuǒbian) — n. — left side — *左(left) + 边(side) — the left side.*
  → 左边那个红色的是我的。— Zuǒbian nàge hóngsè de shì wǒ de. — The red one on the left is mine.

- **红色** (hóngsè) — n. — red — *红(red) + 色(color).*
  → same sentence as 左边.

- **红** (hóng) — adj. — red — same root as 红色.

## Lesson 4 — 这个工作是他帮我介绍的 (He recommended me for this job)

- **生日** (shēngrì) — n. — birthday — *生(born) + 日(day) — the day you were born.*
  → 生日快乐！— Shēngrì kuàilè! — Happy birthday!

- **快乐** (kuàilè) — adj. — happy, glad — *快(fast/pleasant) + 乐(joy) — a joyful feeling.*
  → same sentence as 生日.

- **给** (gěi) — prep. — to, for — *Already touched on in HSK1 — used before a verb to mark the recipient.*
  → 这是送给你的！— Zhè shì sòng gěi nǐ de! — This is for you!

- **接** (jiē) — v. — to receive, take, accept — *扌(hand) radical — reaching out to take/receive something.*
  → 是儿子接的。— Shì érzi jiē de. — Our son answered it.

- **晚上** (wǎnshang) — n. — evening, night — *晚(late) + 上(period) — the late period of the day.*
  → 晚上我问一下儿子。— Wǎnshang wǒ wèn yíxià érzi. — I'll ask him about it this evening.

- **问** (wèn) — v. — to ask — *门(door) + 口(mouth) — asking a question at someone's door, already seen in HSK1.*
  → same sentence as 晚上.

- **非常** (fēicháng) — adv. — very, extremely — *非(not) + 常(ordinary) — "not ordinary," i.e. extremely.*
  → 非常喜欢。— Fēicháng xǐhuan. — Yes, very much (extremely fond).

- **开始** (kāishǐ) — v. — to begin, start — *开(open) + 始(start) — opening/starting something.*
  → 你是什么时候开始踢足球的？— Nǐ shì shénme shíhou kāishǐ tī zúqiú de? — When did you begin to play football?

- **已经** (yǐjīng) — adv. — already — *已(already) + 经(pass through) — having already passed through/happened.*
  → 已经踢了十年了。— Yǐjīng tī le shí nián le. — It has been 10 years already.

- **长** (cháng) — adj. — long — *A simple, elongated character shape — visually "long."*
  → 你在这儿工作多长时间了？— Nǐ zài zhèr gōngzuò duō cháng shíjiān le? — How long have you been working here?

- **两** (liǎng) — num. — two — *Already known from HSK1 — used before measure words instead of 二.*
  → 已经两年多了。— Yǐjīng liǎng nián duō le. — More than two years already.

- **帮** (bāng) — v. — to help, assist — *帛(cloth) + 巾(cloth) — historically about lending material aid.*
  → 这个工作是他帮我介绍的。— Zhège gōngzuò shì tā bāng wǒ jièshào de. — He recommended me for this job.

- **介绍** (jièshào) — v. — to introduce, recommend — *介(between) + 绍(connect) — connecting people, i.e. introducing.*
  → same sentence as 帮.

## Lesson 5 — 就买这件吧 (Take this one)

- **外面** (wàimiàn) — n. — outside — *外(outside) + 面(side/surface) — the outer side.*
  → 我不想去外面吃，我想在家吃。— Wǒ bù xiǎng qù wàimiàn chī, wǒ xiǎng zài jiā chī. — I don't want to eat out, I want to eat at home.

- **准备** (zhǔnbèi) — v. — to prepare, to plan — *准(standard/accurate) + 备(prepare) — preparing something properly.*
  → 那你准备做什么呢？— Nà nǐ zhǔnbèi zuò shénme ne? — What are you planning to cook?

- **就** (jiù) — adv. — (indicates a conclusion/resolution) — *A short decisive word — "just do it," settling on something.*
  → 就做你爱吃的鱼吧。— Jiù zuò nǐ ài chī de yú ba. — I'm thinking of fish, your favorite.

- **鱼** (yú) — n. — fish — *A classic pictograph — the character itself resembles a fish shape.*
  → same sentence as 就.

- **吧** (ba) — part. — (suggestion/consultation particle) — *Already known from HSK1 — softens a sentence into a suggestion.*
  → same sentence as 就.

- **件** (jiàn) — m. — (measure word for clothes/items) — *Used for garments — "a piece of clothing."*
  → 帮我看一下这件衣服怎么样。— Bāng wǒ kàn yíxià zhè jiàn yīfu zěnmeyàng. — What do you think of this garment?

- **还** (hái) — adv. — passably, fairly, rather — *Sounds like "hi" — "eh, it's alright I guess."*
  → 颜色还可以。— Yánsè hái kěyǐ. — The color is OK (passable).

- **可以** (kěyǐ) — adj. — not bad, alright — *可(can/may) + 以(by means of) — acceptable.*
  → same sentence as 还.

- **不错** (búcuò) — adj. — pretty good — *不(not) + 错(wrong) — literally "not wrong," i.e. good.*
  → 这件不错，就买这件吧。— Zhè jiàn búcuò, jiù mǎi zhè jiàn ba. — This one is good. Take this one.

- **考试** (kǎoshì) — n. — test, exam — *考(test) + 试(try) — testing/trying one's knowledge.*
  → 是在想昨天的考试吗？— Shì zài xiǎng zuótiān de kǎoshì ma? — Are you thinking about yesterday's test?

- **意思** (yìsi) — n. — meaning — *意(idea) + 思(thought) — the idea/thought behind a word.*
  → 我都不知道是什么意思。— Wǒ dōu bù zhīdào shì shénme yìsi. — I didn't know the meanings of many characters.

- **咖啡** (kāfēi) — n. — coffee — *Phonetic borrowing from English "coffee" — sounds nearly identical.*
  → 喝咖啡吗？— Hē kāfēi ma? — Would you like some coffee?

- **对** (duì) — prep. — to, for (marking a target/relation) — *Already known from HSK1 as "correct" — here marking "toward/regarding."*
  → 咖啡喝多了对身体不好。— Kāfēi hē duō le duì shēntǐ bù hǎo. — Too much coffee is bad for our health.

- **以后** (yǐhòu) — n. — after, afterwards, later — *以(by) + 后(after) — from this point onward.*
  → 以后我少喝一点儿。— Yǐhòu wǒ shǎo hē yìdiǎnr. — I'll drink less coffee from now on.

## Lesson 6 — 你怎么不吃了 (Why don't you eat more)

- **门** (mén) — n. — door, gate — *A pictograph of double doors.*
  → 我在门外看见他的自行车了。— Wǒ zài mén wài kànjiàn tā de zìxíngchē le. — I saw his bike outside the door.

- **外** (wài) — n. — outer, outside — *夕(evening) + 卜(divination) historically — now simply "outside."*
  → same sentence as 门.

- **自行车** (zìxíngchē) — n. — bike — *自行(self-propelled) + 车(vehicle) — a vehicle that moves itself.*
  → same sentence as 门.

- **羊肉** (yángròu) — n. — mutton — *羊(sheep) + 肉(meat) — meat from a sheep.*
  → 今天的羊肉很好吃，你怎么不吃了？— Jīntiān de yángròu hěn hǎochī, nǐ zěnme bù chī le? — The mutton today is excellent, why don't you eat more?

- **好吃** (hǎochī) — adj. — delicious — *好(good) + 吃(eat) — already known from HSK1.*
  → same sentence as 羊肉.

- **面条** (miàntiáo) — n. — noodles — *面(flour) + 条(strip) — strips made of flour.*
  → 来一点儿面条吧。— Lái yìdiǎnr miàntiáo ba. — Some noodles please.

- **打篮球** (dǎ lánqiú) — to play basketball — *打(hit/play) + 篮球(basket-ball).*
  → 昨天你们怎么都没去打篮球？— Zuótiān nǐmen zěnme dōu méi qù dǎ lánqiú? — Why didn't you guys go to play basketball yesterday?

- **因为** (yīnwèi) — conj. — because, since — *因(cause) + 为(for) — for the cause/reason of.*
  → 因为昨天下雨，所以我们都没去。— Yīnwèi zuótiān xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒmen dōu méi qù. — Because it rained yesterday, so we didn't go.

- **所以** (suǒyǐ) — conj. — so, therefore — *所(that which) + 以(by means of) — the resulting outcome.*
  → same sentence as 因为.

- **游泳** (yóuyǒng) — v. — to swim — *游(swim/roam) + 泳(swim) — doubled for "swimming."*
  → 我去游泳了。— Wǒ qù yóuyǒng le. — I went swimming.

- **经常** (jīngcháng) — adv. — often, frequently — *经(pass through) + 常(usual) — usually passing through/happening.*
  → 你经常游泳吗？— Nǐ jīngcháng yóuyǒng ma? — Do you often swim?

- **公斤** (gōngjīn) — m. — kilogram — *公(public/standard) + 斤(traditional weight unit) — the standardized metric unit.*
  → 我现在七十公斤了。— Wǒ xiànzài qīshí gōngjīn le. — I weigh 70 kilograms now.

- **姐姐** (jiějie) — n. — elder sister — *Repeated syllable, like "jyeh-jyeh" — a warm family term.*
  → 是去看他姐姐。— Shì qù kàn tā jiějie. — Visiting his elder sister.

## Lesson 7 — 你家离公司远吗 (Do you live far from your company)

- **教室** (jiàoshì) — n. — classroom — *教(teach) + 室(room) — the room where teaching happens.*
  → 他还在教室学习呢。— Tā hái zài jiàoshì xuéxí ne. — He is still studying in the classroom.

- **机场** (jīchǎng) — n. — airport — *机(machine/aircraft) + 场(field/venue) — the venue for aircraft.*
  → 在去机场的路上。— Zài qù jīchǎng de lùshang. — I'm on the way to the airport.

- **路** (lù) — n. — road, path, way — *足(foot) radical — the path your feet travel.*
  → same sentence as 机场.

- **离** (lí) — v. — to be away from — *Used to express distance from a place, time, or purpose.*
  → 你家离公司远吗？— Nǐ jiā lí gōngsī yuǎn ma? — Do you live far from your company?

- **公司** (gōngsī) — n. — company, firm — *公(public) + 司(manage) — a publicly managed enterprise.*
  → same sentence as 离.

- **远** (yuǎn) — adj. — far, distant — *辶(walk) radical — a distance you'd have to walk far to cover.*
  → 很远。— Hěn yuǎn. — Yes, very far.

- **公共汽车** (gōnggòng qìchē) — n. — bus — *公共(public) + 汽车(automobile) — a public automobile, i.e. bus.*
  → 坐公共汽车要一个多小时呢。— Zuò gōnggòng qìchē yào yí ge duō xiǎoshí ne. — It takes more than one hour by bus.

- **小时** (xiǎoshí) — n. — hour — *小(small) + 时(time) — a "small" unit of time.*
  → same sentence as 公共汽车.

- **慢** (màn) — adj. — slow — *忄(heart) radical — a heart that takes its time.*
  → 坐公共汽车太慢了。— Zuò gōnggòng qìchē tài màn le. — Buses are too slow.

- **快** (kuài) — adj. — quick, fast — *忄(heart) radical too — a heart racing fast.*
  → 开车也不快。— Kāichē yě bú kuài. — It's not fast either.

- **过** (guò) — v. — to pass (time), to spend (time) — *辶(walk) radical — time passing/walking by.*
  → 给你过生日。— Gěi nǐ guò shēngrì. — Celebrate your birthday.

- **走** (zǒu) — v. — to walk — *A pictograph of legs in motion.*
  → 走几分钟就到了。— Zǒu jǐ fēnzhōng jiù dào le. — Only a few minutes' walk away.

- **到** (dào) — v. — to arrive, reach — *刂(knife) + 至(arrive) historically — now simply "to arrive."*
  → same sentence as 走.

## Lesson 8 — 让我想想再告诉你 (Let me think about it and I'll tell you later)

- **再** (zài) — adv. — again, once more — *A stacked character shape — "one more time on top."*
  → 下午再去吧。— Xiàwǔ zài qù ba. — Let's go again tomorrow afternoon.

- **让** (ràng) — v. — to let, to allow — *讠(speech) radical — telling/allowing someone to do something.*
  → 让我想想再告诉你。— Ràng wǒ xiǎngxiang zài gàosu nǐ. — Let me think about it and I'll tell you later.

- **告诉** (gàosu) — v. — to tell — *告(tell) + 诉(inform) — doubled for "to inform."*
  → same sentence as 让.

- **等** (děng) — v. — to wait, to await — *竹(bamboo) radical historically — patience like waiting bamboo to grow.*
  → 你等等我，好吗？— Nǐ děngdeng wǒ, hǎo ma? — Please wait for me for a minute, will you?

- **找** (zhǎo) — v. — to look for — *扌(hand) radical — reaching out to search for something.*
  → 找大卫有什么事情吗？— Zhǎo Dàwèi yǒu shénme shìqing ma? — What's the matter, looking for David?

- **事情** (shìqing) — n. — thing, matter, affair — *事(matter) + 情(circumstance) — the circumstances of a matter.*
  → same sentence as 找.

- **服务员** (fúwùyuán) — n. — attendant, waiter/waitress — *服务(serve) + 员(member/person) — a person who serves.*
  → 服务员，我房间的门打不开了。— Fúwùyuán, wǒ fángjiān de mén dǎ bu kāi le. — Excuse me, I can't open my door.

- **白** (bái) — adj. — white — *A simple pictograph resembling a white sun/light.*
  → 这件白的有点儿长。— Zhè jiàn bái de yǒudiǎnr cháng. — This white one is a little too long.

- **黑** (hēi) — adj. — black — *灬(fire) radical at the bottom — soot from fire, black.*
  → 那件黑的有点儿贵。— Nà jiàn hēi de yǒudiǎnr guì. — That black one is a bit expensive.

- **贵** (guì) — adj. — expensive — *贝(shell/money) radical — money-related, i.e. costly.*
  → same sentence as 黑.

## Lesson 9 — 题太多，我没做完 (There were too many questions; I didn't finish all of them)

- **错** (cuò) — adj. — wrong, incorrect — *钅(metal) radical historically — now simply "mistaken."*
  → 你打错了。— Nǐ dǎ cuò le. — You've got the wrong number.

- **从** (cóng) — prep. — from — *Two 人(person) side by side — one following from another, "from."*
  → 您从几岁开始学习跳舞？— Nín cóng jǐ suì kāishǐ xuéxí tiàowǔ? — At what age did you start to learn dancing?

- **跳舞** (tiàowǔ) — v. — to dance — *跳(jump) + 舞(dance) — dancing involves jumping/moving.*
  → same sentence as 从.

- **第一** (dì yī) — num. — first — *第(ordinal prefix) + 一(one) — "number one," first.*
  → 我第一次跳舞是在七岁的时候。— Wǒ dì yī cì tiàowǔ shì zài qī suì de shíhou. — I was seven when I danced for the first time.

- **希望** (xīwàng) — v. — to hope, to wish — *希(hope) + 望(gaze/expect) — gazing hopefully forward.*
  → 我希望她能跟您学跳舞。— Wǒ xīwàng tā néng gēn nín xué tiàowǔ. — I hope she can learn to dance from you.

- **问题** (wèntí) — n. — question, problem — *问(ask) + 题(topic) — a topic that's being asked about.*
  → 没问题，非常欢迎。— Méi wèntí, fēicháng huānyíng. — No problem, sure, my pleasure.

- **欢迎** (huānyíng) — v. — to welcome — *欢(joy) + 迎(greet) — greeting someone joyfully.*
  → same sentence as 问题.

- **上班** (shàng bān) — v. — to work, to do a job — *上(go to) + 班(shift/work team) — going to one's work shift.*
  → 他从什么时候开始上班？— Tā cóng shénme shíhou kāishǐ shàng bān? — When will he start to work?

- **懂** (dǒng) — v. — to understand, to know — *忄(heart) radical — understanding felt in the heart.*
  → 你都听懂了吗？— Nǐ dōu tīngdǒng le ma? — Did you understand everything you heard?

- **完** (wán) — v. — to finish, to end — *宀(roof) radical — completing something under one roof.*
  → 你都做完了没有？— Nǐ dōu zuòwán le méiyǒu? — Did you finish the test paper?

- **题** (tí) — n. — question, problem — *页(page/head) radical — a question written on a page.*
  → 题太多，我没做完。— Tí tài duō, wǒ méi zuòwán. — There were too many questions, I didn't finish all of them.

## Lesson 10 — 别找了，手机在桌子上呢 (Stop looking for your cell phone; it's on the desk)

- **课** (kè) — n. — class, lesson — *讠(speech) radical — a class involves speech/teaching.*
  → 明天上午还有汉语课呢。— Míngtiān shàngwǔ hái yǒu Hànyǔ kè ne. — You'll have a Chinese class tomorrow morning.

- **帮助** (bāngzhù) — v. — to help, assist — *帮(help) + 助(aid) — doubled for emphasis.*
  → 看电视对学汉语有帮助。— Kàn diànshì duì xué Hànyǔ yǒu bāngzhù. — Watching TV is good for Chinese learning.

- **别** (bié) — adv. — don't — *A short, sharp word for prohibition — "别!" = "don't!"*
  → 别看报纸了。— Bié kàn bàozhǐ le. — Stop reading the newspaper.

- **哥哥** (gēge) — n. — elder brother — *Repeated syllable, a warm family term like 姐姐.*
  → 哥哥今天中午回来吃饭。— Gēge jīntiān zhōngwǔ huílai chī fàn. — Our elder brother will come back to have lunch.

- **鸡蛋** (jīdàn) — n. — (hen's) egg — *鸡(chicken) + 蛋(egg) — an egg from a chicken.*
  → 羊肉、鸡蛋、面条、西瓜。— Yángròu, jīdàn, miàntiáo, xīguā. — Mutton, eggs, noodles, watermelon.

- **西瓜** (xīguā) — n. — watermelon — *西(west) + 瓜(melon) — historically a melon introduced from the west.*
  → same sentence as 鸡蛋.

- **正在** (zhèngzài) — adv. — in the process of — *正(right now) + 在(at) — right at this moment.*
  → 正在准备午饭呢！— Zhèngzài zhǔnbèi wǔfàn ne! — She is preparing lunch right now!

- **手机** (shǒujī) — n. — cell phone — *手(hand) + 机(machine) — a machine you hold in hand.*
  → 你看见我的手机了吗？— Nǐ kànjiàn wǒ de shǒujī le ma? — Have you seen my cell phone?

- **洗** (xǐ) — v. — to wash — *氵(water) radical — washing involves water.*
  → 那件衣服我帮你洗了。— Nà jiàn yīfu wǒ bāng nǐ xǐ le. — I have washed it for you.

## Lesson 11 — 他比我大三岁 (He is three years older than me)

- **唱歌** (chànggē) — v. — to sing — *唱(sing) + 歌(song) — singing a song.*
  → 昨天和你一起唱歌的人是谁？— Zuótiān hé nǐ yìqǐ chànggē de rén shì shéi? — Who was the guy with you in the karaoke yesterday?

- **男** (nán) — adj. — man, male — *田(field) + 力(strength) — historically, a man's strength in the fields.*
  → 是不是男朋友？— Shì bu shì nánpéngyou? — Boyfriend?

- **女** (nǚ) — adj. — woman, female — *A pictograph of a kneeling figure, the character for "woman."*
  → 左边这个看报纸的女孩子是你姐姐吗？— Zuǒbian zhège kàn bàozhǐ de nǚ háizi shì nǐ jiějie ma? — Is the girl on the left reading a newspaper your elder sister?

- **孩子** (háizi) — n. — child, kid — *孩(child) + 子(suffix) — the general word for "child."*
  → same sentence as 女.

- **右边** (yòubian) — n. — right side — *右(right) + 边(side) — the right side.*
  → 右边写字的那个人是我哥哥。— Yòubian xiě zì de nàge rén shì wǒ gēge. — The guy on the right writing is my elder brother.

- **比** (bǐ) — prep. — than (used for comparison) — *Two similar strokes side by side — comparing two things.*
  → 他比我大三岁。— Tā bǐ wǒ dà sān suì. — He is three years older than me.

- **便宜** (piányi) — adj. — cheap, inexpensive — *便(convenient) + 宜(suitable) — conveniently affordable.*
  → 比昨天便宜。— Bǐ zuótiān piányi. — It's cheaper than yesterday.

- **说话** (shuōhuà) — v. — to speak, talk — *说(speak) + 话(words) — speaking words.*
  → 前边说话的那个人就是我的汉语老师。— Qiánbian shuōhuà de nàge rén jiù shì wǒ de Hànyǔ lǎoshī. — The woman speaking in front of us is my Chinese teacher.

- **可能** (kěnéng) — aux. — maybe, perhaps — *可(can) + 能(able) — it's "possible/able" to be so.*
  → 你可能不认识她。— Nǐ kěnéng bú rènshi tā. — You may not know her.

- **去年** (qùnián) — n. — last year — *去(past/gone) + 年(year) — the year that's passed.*
  → 是去年来的。— Shì qùnián lái de. — She came here last year.

- **姓** (xìng) — v. — to have the family name of — *女(woman) + 生(birth) — historically tied to matrilineal lineage.*
  → 她姓王。— Tā xìng Wáng. — Her family name is Wang.

## Lesson 12 — 你穿得太少了 (You wear too little)

- **得** (de) — part. — (introduces a complement of result/degree after a verb/adjective) — *A grammatical particle, no independent meaning — links an action to its result.*
  → 你吃得太少了。— Nǐ chī de tài shǎo le. — You ate too little.

- **妻子** (qīzi) — n. — wife — *妻(wife) + 子(suffix) — the general word for "wife."*
  → 我妻子比我做得好。— Wǒ qīzi bǐ wǒ zuò de hǎo. — My wife is a better cook than I am.

- **雪** (xuě) — n. — snow — *雨(rain) radical on top — frozen rain, i.e. snow.*
  → 下雪了，今天真冷。— Xià xuě le, jīntiān zhēn lěng. — It's snowing, it's really cold today.

- **零** (líng) — num. — zero — *雨(rain) radical — like scattered raindrops, "zero" as in scattered/nothing.*
  → 有零下10度吧？— Yǒu líng xià shí dù ba? — The temperature may be 10 degrees below zero, am I right?

- **度** (dù) — n. — degree — *广(shelter) + 又(hand) historically — a measured unit, now "degree."*
  → same sentence as 零.

- **穿** (chuān) — v. — to wear, put on — *穴(hole/cave) + 牙(tooth) historically — literally to "pierce through," now "to wear."*
  → 你穿得太少了。— Nǐ chuān de tài shǎo le. — You wear too little.

- **进** (jìn) — v. — to enter — *辶(walk) radical — walking into a place.*
  → 我们进房间吧。— Wǒmen jìn fángjiān ba. — Let's go inside the house.

- **弟弟** (dìdi) — n. — younger brother — *Repeated syllable, family term like 哥哥/姐姐.*
  → 我弟弟让我帮他找个房子。— Wǒ dìdi ràng wǒ bāng tā zhǎo ge fángzi. — My younger brother asked me to find an apartment for him.

- **近** (jìn) — adj. — near, close — *辶(walk) radical — a short walk away.*
  → 他也希望能住得近一点儿。— Tā yě xīwàng néng zhù de jìn yìdiǎnr. — He also wants to live nearer.

## Lesson 13 — 门开着呢 (The door is open)

- **着** (zhe) — part. — (indicates a continuing state) — *A grammatical particle attached after a verb to show an ongoing state.*
  → 门开着呢，请进。— Mén kāi zhe ne, qǐng jìn. — The door is open. Come on in.

- **手** (shǒu) — n. — hand — *A pictograph of a hand with fingers.*
  → 那个手里拿着铅笔的呢？— Nàge shǒu li názhe qiānbǐ de ne? — What about the girl with a pencil in her hand?

- **拿** (ná) — v. — to hold, to take — *合(combine) + 手(hand) — combining/gripping with the hand.*
  → same sentence as 手.

- **铅笔** (qiānbǐ) — n. — pencil — *铅(lead) + 笔(writing instrument) — a lead-based writing instrument.*
  → same sentence as 手.

- **班** (bān) — n. — class, team, grade — *王(king/jade) + 刂(knife) historically — a divided group, now "class."*
  → 就是我们班那个长着两个大眼睛的女孩儿。— Jiù shì wǒmen bān nàge zhǎngzhe liǎng ge dà yǎnjing de nǚháir. — She is the girl in our class with big eyes.

- **长** (zhǎng) — v. — to grow, to develop — *Same character as "长(cháng, long)" but different tone/meaning — growing "long."*
  → same sentence as 班.

- **笑** (xiào) — v. — to smile, to laugh — *竹(bamboo) radical on top — bamboo bending like a smiling face.*
  → 非常爱笑的女孩儿。— Fēicháng ài xiào de nǚháir. — and a girl who smiles a lot.

- **宾馆** (bīnguǎn) — n. — hotel — *宾(guest) + 馆(building/hall) — a building for guests.*
  → 这儿离新京宾馆远吗？— Zhèr lí Xīnjīng Bīnguǎn yuǎn ma? — Is Xinjing Hotel far away from here?

- **一直** (yìzhí) — adv. — straight, all along — *一(one) + 直(straight) — going straight, continuously.*
  → 从这儿一直往前走。— Cóng zhèr yìzhí wǎng qián zǒu. — Walk straight ahead from here.

- **往** (wǎng) — prep. — towards — *彳(step) radical — stepping toward a direction.*
  → same sentence as 一直.

- **路口** (lùkǒu) — n. — crossing, crossroads — *路(road) + 口(mouth/opening) — the opening where roads meet.*
  → 到了前面的路口再往右走。— Dàole qiánmiàn de lùkǒu zài wǎng yòu zǒu. — Turn right at the first crossing.

## Lesson 14 — 你看过那个电影吗 (Have you seen that movie)

- **有意思** (yǒu yìsi) — adj. — interesting, fun — *有(have) + 意思(meaning) — literally "has meaning," i.e. interesting.*
  → 听说很有意思。— Tīngshuō hěn yǒu yìsi. — I was told it is fantastic.

- **但是** (dànshì) — conj. — but, still, yet — *但(but) + 是(is) — a standard contrast conjunction.*
  → 可以，但是我女朋友也想去。— Kěyǐ, dànshì wǒ nǚpéngyou yě xiǎng qù. — OK, but my girlfriend also wants to go.

- **虽然** (suīrán) — conj. — although, though — *虽(although) + 然(so) — "even though it is so."*
  → 我虽然去过好几次，但是还想再去玩儿玩儿。— Wǒ suīrán qùguo hǎojǐ cì, dànshì hái xiǎng zài qù wánrwanr. — Though I've been there several times, I still want to go again.

- **次** (cì) — m. — (measure word for frequency/times) — *冫(ice) + 欠(lack) historically — now the standard counter for "times."*
  → same sentence as 虽然.

- **玩儿** (wánr) — v. — to play, have fun — *王(king/jade) radical — playing with jade toys historically.*
  → same sentence as 虽然.

- **晴** (qíng) — adj. — sunny, clear — *日(sun) radical — sun-related, i.e. clear weather.*
  → 虽然是晴天，但是很冷。— Suīrán shì qíngtiān, dànshì hěn lěng. — It will be fine, but cold.

- **百** (bǎi) — num. — hundred — *白(white) + 一(one) historically — the character for "hundred."*
  → 两百块还可以。— Liǎngbǎi kuài hái kěyǐ. — Two hundred yuan is not that expensive.

## Lesson 15 — 新年就要到了 (The New Year is coming)

- **日** (rì) — n. — day, date — *A pictograph of the sun — a day marked by the sun.*
  → 今天是12月20日。— Jīntiān shì shí'èr yuè èrshí rì. — Today is December 20th.

- **新年** (xīnnián) — n. — New Year — *新(new) + 年(year) — the new year.*
  → 新年就要到了。— Xīnnián jiù yào dào le. — The New Year is coming.

- **票** (piào) — n. — ticket — *示(show) radical historically — a paper you show to prove entry.*
  → 你买票了吗？— Nǐ mǎi piào le ma? — Have you bought the ticket?

- **火车站** (huǒchēzhàn) — n. — railway station — *火车(train, lit. "fire vehicle") + 站(station) — the station for trains.*
  → 明天就去火车站买票。— Míngtiān jiù qù huǒchēzhàn mǎi piào. — I'll go to the railway station to buy it tomorrow.

- **大家** (dàjiā) — pron. — all, everybody — *大(big) + 家(family/household) — everyone in the big group.*
  → 谢谢大家这一年对我的帮助！— Xièxie dàjiā zhè yì nián duì wǒ de bāngzhù! — Thank you all for your help during the past year!

- **更** (gèng) — adv. — more, to a greater extent — *A comparative adverb — "even more so."*
  → 希望我们的公司明年更好！— Xīwàng wǒmen de gōngsī míngnián gèng hǎo! — I hope our company will become even better next year!

- **妹妹** (mèimei) — n. — younger sister — *Repeated syllable, family term like 姐姐/哥哥/弟弟.*
  → 你妹妹怎么还没来？— Nǐ mèimei zěnme hái méi lái? — Why hasn't your younger sister arrived yet?

- **阴** (yīn) — adj. — overcast, cloudy — *阝(mound/shade) + 月(moon) — the shaded, moonlit, overcast side.*
  → 天阴了，我要回去了。— Tiān yīn le, wǒ yào huíqu le. — It's overcast, I have to go home.
